# Genealogy & ancestry — world-class research knowledge

Load in Phases 1–3 (locality + planning + searching) and whenever identity, parentage, origin, or migration is in dispute. This is the professional body of knowledge behind the skill — BCG *Genealogy Standards*, Elizabeth Shown Mills' evidence framework (*Evidence Explained*, *Historical Biography*), Thomas W. Jones' *Mastering Genealogical Proof*, and the methods taught at IGHR, SLIG, and British/Irish/Australian institutes. **Mastery is knowing which record answers which question, how jurisdictions actually worked, and how proof is built — not memorising dates.**

## Proof architecture (what world-class looks like)

- **Question-first.** Every hour serves one written research question. Biography and context support identity; they do not replace vitals, census, land, and probate for parentage.
- **Five GPS elements** are a minimum bar, not a ceiling: exhaustive means the matrix + FAN + passes in `exhaustive-research.md`, not "I tried FamilySearch."
- **Evidence is a matrix, not a stack.** Correlate indirect items (census age + baptism + probate naming heir + land deed + neighbour FAN) until the pattern is stronger than any single direct statement — many direct statements are wrong (informant error, step-parent named as parent).
- **Citation granularity:** cite the **specific** image/entry (roll, page, line, certificate number), not the database homepage. Future you must re-find it in ten years.
- **Authored works** (trees, DAR lines, county histories) are finding aids. The proof lives in originals you examined.

## Identity & kinship — expert moves

- **FAN club** (Elizabeth Shown Mills): Family, Associates, Neighbours — plus witnesses, sponsors, executors, bondsmen, fellow passengers, godparents, in-laws, guardians. In pre-1800 research, FAN often *is* the primary method.
- **Cluster research:** treat a household, street, ship, regiment, or church pew as a unit; one member's record explains another's.
- **Female lines:** search under maiden and married names, husband's records, children's records, widow's pension, father's probate naming married daughters, sister's marriage record.
- **Same-name systems:** build a **candidate table** (P### per person) with disambiguating facts; never collapse until the same-person test passes (see `methods.md`).
- **Boundaries:** always research the event at **jurisdiction-as-was** — parish, hundred, townland, civil district, enumeration district. Use gazetteers (GB1900, NLS maps, USGS GNIS, GeoNames) and historical atlases.
- **Calendars & dating:** Julian/Gregorian, regnal years, feast-day dating, French Republican, Quaker numbering, Jewish calendar — convert in the analysis layer, preserve original in transcription.
- **Naming systems:** patronymics (Scandinavia, Wales, Netherlands), Spanish/Portuguese dual surnames, French dit names, Irish agnomen, Ashkenazi naming patterns (hint only), farm names (Norway) — drive variant lists in `01-plan.md`.

## Record-type mastery — what experts mine (beyond vitals)

| Domain | Expert use | Pro tips |
|---|---|---|
| **Land & property** | Prove residence, kin ("heirs of"), neighbours, economic status, migration | Metes-and-bounds vs lot/section; grantor **and** grantee indexes; federal land (BLM-GLO) vs state/county deeds; mortgage = debt trail; partition suits name all heirs |
| **Probate & succession** | Death proof, children (incl. married daughters), in-laws, prior spouse | Wills, administrations, inventories, guardianships, orphan's court, prerogative/high court for boundary cases; **all** heirs sign quitclaims in land records |
| **Tax & fiscal** | Year-by-year presence/absence, rough wealth, occupation | Personal vs real property; poll tax = males of age; missing years = moved or dead |
| **Court** | Kinship in disputes, bastardy bonds, debt, divorce, naturalisation of character | Quarter sessions, chancery/equity (family disputes), criminal calendars in newspapers |
| **Church** | Baptism/marriage/burial; membership, transfers, discipline, sponsors | Sponsors/godparents often kin; kirk session (Scotland); Quaker monthly minutes; Jewish ketubot/mohel records via community archives |
| **Military** | Age, birthplace, next-of-kin, physical description, unit | **Pension files** (widow/parent/child applications) are among the richest kinship sources in US/UK/Canada/Australia; draft cards; service records + regimental histories |
| **Migration** | Origin town, ship, chain migration | Departure *and* arrival lists; passport; naturalisation (US post-1906 detail); alien registration; hometown societies and obits naming "native of" |
| **Newspapers** | Obit, marriage, court, land, accident, society, shipping, ads | Treat as core Phase 3 pass, not garnish; ethnic/religious press; "Information wanted" (Boston *Pilot*, etc.) |
| **Directories & occupational** | Address timeline, occupation, widow entries, employer | City directories year-by-year; professional registers; guild/union; alumni |
| **Institutional** | Parents, birthplace, next-of-kin | Workhouse, asylum, prison, school, orphan train, indenture — often only place a birth is stated |
| **Census & substitutes** | Household reconstruction, FAN on the page | US 1850–1950; UK/Ireland fragments; state censuses; colonial musters; tithe/griffith substitutes |

## Geography & repository fluency (verify current access)

**North America:** NARA + Fold3; FamilySearch; Ancestry/MyHeritage; state archives & county clerks; **Library and Archives Canada** / BAnQ; **Mexican** parish/civil via FamilySearch; **Caribbean** colonial records fragmented — plan by colony.

**British Isles:** **TNA** (England/Wales national); county record offices; **Scotland's People** (pay-per-view statutory + OPR + kirk session); **PRONI** / **NLI** / **IrishGenealogy**; **GRO** certificates; **BMD registers** vs parish registers; **Registry of Deeds** (Ireland); **Valuation Office**.

**Continental Europe:** Parish books + civil Standesamt (privacy windows); **Archion**, **Matricula**, departmental archives (France), **WieWasWie** (Netherlands), **ArkivDigital** / **Digitalarkivet** (Scandinavia), **Geneteka** / **PTG** (Poland), **FamilySearch** microfilm legacy.

**Jewish:** **JewishGen**, **JRI-Poland**, **Gesher Galicia**, **YIVO**, community kehillah records — many destroyed; never infer Holocaust survival from absence.

**African American & slavery:** Freedmen's Bureau, Freedman's Bank, USCT pensions, estate/inventory/sale records — interpret with **Beyond Kin** and descendant-community ethics (`ethics-and-privacy.md`).

**Indigenous:** Tribal/nation archives only; citizenship is political, not DNA (`ethics-and-privacy.md`).

**Australia/NZ:** **NAA**, **BDM** registries, **Trove**, **Papers Past**, convict records.

Use **FamilySearch Catalog**, **ArchiveGrid**, **WorldCat**, and national archive finding aids to locate **unindexed** holdings — experts order microfilm/digital copies and browse.

## Ancestry vs genealogy (skill uses both words correctly)

- **Genealogy** = provable parentage and kinship (GPS).
- **Ancestry** = the full line of descent including contexts (ethnic origin, social class, migration story) — may include `PROBABLE`/`POSSIBLE` threads clearly labelled.
- **Ethnicity estimates** from DNA companies are not ancestry proof for a specific ancestor; admixture is `SPECULATIVE` context (`dna.md`, `statistics.md`).
- **Heraldry/coats of arms** sold online are not proof of medieval descent; document each generation or label decorative.

## Advanced integration

- **One-place / one-name studies:** check if the parish/surname is already studied — cite the study, re-verify in originals.
- **Genetic + documentary:** build documentary trees for matches first; DNA narrows hypotheses; WATO with all plausible hypotheses (`dna.md`).
- **Historical context** informs *where* to search (Famine migration timing, Homestead Act, indentured servants, convict transport) — never substitutes for a record.
- **Language:** church Latin, German Gothic script, French civil formulae — paleography reference; hire/community translator when out of depth, log as `blocked`.

## Quality bar before you claim world-class output

Could a BCG-certified genealogist re-run your log and reach the same conclusion? Would they find gaps in your FAN sweep, your jurisdictions, or your citations? If yes — keep working the backlog. If the conclusion is right but GPS incomplete — label honestly and list exactly what remains.
