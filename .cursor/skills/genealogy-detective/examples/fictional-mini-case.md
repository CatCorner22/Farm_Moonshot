# Fictional mini-case — every name, place and record below is invented

Purpose: show the expected discipline on one small question. It is a style/scale model, not research.

**Question:** Who were the parents of *Elias Venn* (P001), b. ~1841 "in Ohio," d. 12 Mar 1911 (invented Cedar County, Ohio)?

## How the phases look at this scale

- **Intake:** User lore: "his father was a German sea captain." → logged as lore, `SPECULATIVE`.
- **Phase 1 (locality):** Cedar County created 1838 from two parent counties → pre-1838 records sit in the parent counties; Ohio civil birth registration starts 1867 → birth proof must come from baptism/census/church substitutes. German Lutheran congregation founded 1845, registers survive.
- **Plan hypotheses:** H1 father = sea captain (lore). H2 father = local farmer of same surname. H0 parents not in Ohio at all (born en route / in Pennsylvania).
- **Exhaustive search (excerpt of log):** 1850–1880 censuses (found 1850, 1860; nil 1870 Cedar Co. — searched spelling variants `Venn/Fenn/Van`, nil logged); Lutheran baptisms 1840–45 (found); probate index 1838–1915 (one hit); deeds (two hits); newspapers 1900–11 (obituary found); WWI draft of grandson (names birthplace of father — indirect).

**Same-person check:** a second Elias Venn (cousin, b. 1844, neighbouring county) was kept as P002 and excluded by spouse, land parcel, and simultaneous census households — noted so no one re-merges him.

**Red-team:** tried to falsify C001 by seeking a death/burial for infant Elias (which would make the adult a different child) — baptism register and burial index searched 1841–50, nil logged; conclusion stands.

**Answer delivered:** parents Matthias Venn & Anna Braun (`PROVEN`), sea-captain lore `DISPROVED`, with the dossier, citations, and one open item (Anna's immigration record — exact FamilySearch collection + years given to the user as a retrieval task).
