Arkansas Marriage Records

Marriage records maintained in the Division of Vital Records begin with January 1917. The Division of Vital Records does not have any marriage records prior to 1917.

Divorce records maintained in the Division of Vital Records begin with January 1923. The Division of Vital Records does not have any divorce records prior to 1923

Express service for records

Marriage (State)

Cost of copy: $10.00

Address:
Division of Vital Records Slot 44
Arkansas Department of Health
4815 West Markham Street
Little Rock, AR 72205-3867

Coupons since 1917. Additional copies of the same marriage record, when requested at the same time, are $10.00 each.

Marriage (county)

Cost of copy: Varies

Full certified copy may be obtained from County Clerk in county where license was issued.
