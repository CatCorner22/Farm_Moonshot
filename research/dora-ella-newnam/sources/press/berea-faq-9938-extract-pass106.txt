Q. Where is the Berea Citizen Index located?

A. The Berea Citizen Index is available in two places. The index for the dates 1899 through 1983 is in paper form on index cards. The card box is located on the main floor of Hutchins Library between rooms 228 and 229 behind the Reference Collection.

In 1984 the Berea Citizen became an independent newspaper. The College, however, continued to index the articles within the newspaper. The index from 1984 to present day can be found online via the link below.
