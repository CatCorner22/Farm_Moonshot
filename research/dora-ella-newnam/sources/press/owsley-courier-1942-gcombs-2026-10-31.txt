Owsley County Courier, Friday, May 8, 1942
"George Combs Killed Wednesday"
George COMBS, Owsley County farmer, was killed by wounds received near Vincent about 7 o'clock Wednesday morning, May 7. According to reports he was shot from ambush.
COMBS, reports on his way to work in a nearby corn field, was shot eleven times with a 32-30 caliber gun.
Survivors: widow Mrs. Ocre COMBS; sons Orear, Jack, Eugene, Russell, Beford, Billy; daughters Leona, Mary Francis, Jennieve.
