Harry GARRETT and Miss Mamie THOMPSON, Denning were married at the court house by Judge Weaver yesterday.the couple drove up from Denning in a car intending to get their license and be married in time to leave for greenwood, Arkansas on the "dinky", but missing their train they went on the afternoon train No#104.
