Answered By: Reference Desk
Last Updated: Jul 21, 2014     Views: 165

Yes. Hutchins Library does provide access to the Berea Citizen in many formats. 

We retain the most recent four issues of the Berea Citizen in print hardcopy. 

We maintain a subscription to a microfilm collection of the Berea Citizen from 1899 to 2008; more recent years are added as they become available. The Berea Citizen for the years1900-1922 is also available electronically: http://chroniclingamerica.loc.gov/lccn/sn85052076/issues/

If you need assistance printing, copying, scanning, and/or emailing Berea Citizen, please ask at the Reference Desk. Microfilm can be difficult to work with at first, but our staff can help you navigate it with ease.
