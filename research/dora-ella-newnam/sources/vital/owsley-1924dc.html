The following Owsley County Ky death certificates extracts were transcribed from photographs. Some of these dc's were hard to read because of fading, ink blobs, bad handwriting, and failing eyesight of the transcriber.  Not all the blanks were always filled in on these certificates and sometimes the informant may have given wrong information.
