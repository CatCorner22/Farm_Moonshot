1900 Lee County Ky Census
Extracted by Debi Houser Kendrick


I've tried to copy this census the best that I could.  The bottoms of some of the pages were so dark on my copies that it was near to impossible to read the names and information found there.  Sometimes, the census taker  wasn't the best speller in the world and made mistakes in writing down some of these names.  For example, in E.D. 47 I noticed that the enumerated spelled the name" Newton" as "Nuton."   I haven't tried to correct any spelling, age or date errors that are on the census and just left them as they appeared.  Happy Hunting!
