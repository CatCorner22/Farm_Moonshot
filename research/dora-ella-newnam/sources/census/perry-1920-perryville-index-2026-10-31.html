Note: The Perryville town/Fourche LaFave twp census begins on page 27a and ends on page 34b. The census taker labels each page as Perryville town and the last two pages, 34a & 34b, as Fourche LaFave twp. I believe that Fourche LaFave twp begins before page 34a but it is difficult to determine exactly where it begins so I have combined the town & township.
