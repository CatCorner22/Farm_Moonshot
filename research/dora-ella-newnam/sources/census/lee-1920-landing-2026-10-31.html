  I know, I know, everyone is asking what took me so long with the last precint of St. Helens.  It seems like several things conspired to keep me from finishing this last bit of Lee County but it's finally done.  What's the old saying, better late than never!

  Some of the households I have added information, such as the maiden name of a woman.  This will appear, for example "nee SMITH".  I've also added a few birthdates for children that I have extracted from  the Ky Birth Index 1911-1920.

I would also like to give a special thanks to Margaret Millar Hayes.  She has been a tremendous help to me in identifying individuals and correcting me when I err.  Thanks Maggie!!
