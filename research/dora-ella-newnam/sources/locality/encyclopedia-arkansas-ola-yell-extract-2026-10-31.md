## Facts used (locality context only — not a Dora record)

- Coordinates 35º01′56″N 093º13′24″W; elevation 361 ft.
- Incorporated **20 March 1900** as a second-class city.
- Name changed from Petit Jean to **Ola** on **10 December 1880** (town named for the first child of John Mathias Harkey).
- Federal population: **1910 = 516**; **1920 = 655**; **1930 = 648**.
- Early 20th century: timber, fruit, cotton; weekly newspaper; doctors; Rock Island after Dardanelle, Ola and Southern (chartered 1 May 1906, completed Aug 1907, part of Rock Island 31 Dec 1911–1938).
- Ola Bottle Works **1920–1928**.
- Methodist church 1880; Southern Baptist 1900.
- Nimrod Lake/Dam nearby (recreation; **not** a statement that Dora lived at Nimrod).
- Additional published leads **not yet examined**: Wayne Banks, *History of Yell County* (1959); Dale S. Sandlin, “History of Ola, Arkansas” (1980 MS, Arkansas River Valley Regional Library, Dardanelle); *Yell County Heritage* (1997).

Do not treat this authored locality page as proof of Dora’s address. Population **655** in 1920 matches the 1921 and 1929 *American Medical Directory* town-header figure for Ola.
