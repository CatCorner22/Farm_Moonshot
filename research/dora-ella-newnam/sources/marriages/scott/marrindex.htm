SCOTT COUNTY MARRIAGE INDEX

BOOKS A - J

This index to the Scott County Marriage Records is provided for your genealogical reseach. You may wish to obtain a copy of the complete record from the County Clerk's office. You should include the names of both the bride and groom, the date, as well as the Book and Page Numbers from this index and a stamped, self-addressed envelope. I do not know the current price for this service.
