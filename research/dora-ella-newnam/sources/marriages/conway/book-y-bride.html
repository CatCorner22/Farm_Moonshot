Terms used:

“No return” means there is no documentation that the marriage occured.  The space where the proof was supposed to be recorded is blank.

“Not executed” means the marriage for that particular license did not happen.  The license was returned to the courthouse and hand-written across the face of the license application are the words: “Cancelled.  Not Executed.”

“None” means the person’s age is blank.

“L/D” means license application date.  Provided for marriage records with ‘no return’ and for marriages ‘not executed’.
