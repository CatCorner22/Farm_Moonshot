Charlie NEWNAM & Eva ROSS
Groom: white, age 20, single, born Lee Co, residence: Idamay, KY, railroader
Bride: white, age 19, single, born Owsley Co, residence: Idamay, KY, housekeeper
Groom's parents: Richard Newnam & Sarah Damerell
Bride's parents: George Ross & Nettie Webb
md 11 Jul 1914 at clerk's office by I. McGuire
witnesses: J. E. Jones & A. S. McGuire

Same page cluster: Lloyd BRANDENBURG & Emma BEGLEY, 24 Oct 1914,
parents Major Brandenburg & Emily Hamilton; witnesses Shelby Newman & Perry Begley.
