Married Life
When I was young I use to think that married life would be
an endless round of kissing and having friends to tea.
Of mornings spending dusting and putting things to right
Then hours of lonely waiting until he came home at night.
But now I know marriage means infinity more
then kisses cakes and coil. It is life's very core.
It is tears and joys and sunshine. It is work and never fame.
It is babies bills and baking but it is him just the the same.
