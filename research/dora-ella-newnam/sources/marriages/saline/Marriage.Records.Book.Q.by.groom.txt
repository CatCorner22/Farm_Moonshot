Saline County, Arkansas Marriage Record Book “Q” 
Arranged Alphabetically by Groom 
 
November 1923 to September 1925 
 
Submitted by George M. Akins 
 
Many contributions were made in transcribing names of groom and bride, dates of marriage, book and page numbers of the actual Saline County 
Marriage Records in the Saline County Courthouse Clerks Office - a copy was donated to the Saline County History and Heritage Society. To this, 
we proofed and added the ages, township/city, county, state and notes for each groom and bride. 
 
Following the name of the groom or bride, the notation ‘(Col)’ sometimes appeared on the application. The clerk was apparently noting that the 
groom or bride was ‘colored.’  We caution you not to assume that this was done consistently throughout the marriage records in the courthouse, but 
for those indicated we are showing in this book. 
 
Following the name of the bride, we have added the courtesy title of ‘Mrs.’ to the brides that have been married, widowed, or divorced. The only 
instance we added the courtesy title ‘Miss’ to a bride was when she was older; we wanted to assure the researcher reading this book that the bride 
was shown to be a single woman on the License. 
 
The ‘YY’ ‘MM’ “DD’ columns indicate year, month and day of marriage. Columns filled with ‘00’ ‘00’ ’00,’ indicate that the License was 
returned, not used, or that it was never brought back to be recorded.  The ‘BP’ column conveys what book and page on which the marriage License 
is recorded. 
 
The ‘Notes’ column contains pieces of information that would be of interest to the researcher (if there was a permission note attached to the 
marriage record for persons under age). For the consent notes, we tried to show who gave consent for the marriage on the note and, when possible, 
we indicated whether it was the mother, father, or both parents signed the note.  We also indicated in this column whether the marriage License was 
returned unused or if one of the parties died before wedding – anything we thought might help explain the record.  The following abbreviations 
appeared in the ‘Notes’ column: 
 
‘br’ = bride                   ‘CoJ’ = County Judge                        ‘CrJ’ Circuit Judge                 ’DNP’= Do Not Publish                      ‘Fa.’ = Father                           
‘gr’ = groom                 ‘JP’ = Justice of the Peace                 ‘md’ = Married                       ‘MG’ = Minister of Gospel                 ‘Mo.’ = Mother
