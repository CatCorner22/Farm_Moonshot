Lee County Marriage Book 10 (1912–1914)
Transcribed by Debi Houser Kendrick from photocopies of the county book.
URL: https://usgennet.org/usa/ky/county/lee/marriage/marrbook10.html
Captured 31 Oct 2026. Transcription only. Original book image not seen.

Shelby NEWNAM & Gracie BRANDENBURG
Groom: age: 21, born: Lee Co KY, residence: Lee Co KY, farming, 1st marriage
Name & Birthplace of Groom's Father: Richard Newnam
Name & Birthplace of Groom's Mother: Sarah Damrell
Bride: age: 15, born: Lee Co KY, residence: Lee Co KY, 1st marriage
Name & Birthplace of Bride's Father: Major Brandenburg
Name & Birthplace of Bride's Mother: Emily Howelton
md: 4 Sept 1913 at bride's home, Lee Co, by J. H. Cecil, J.P.L.C.
witnesses: W. M. Burke & Dema Newnam

Note: mother's surname is written Howelton on this return. The 1920 Sturgeon
transcript (hh 66-71) lists Major Brandenburg's wife as Emma nee HAMILTON.
Do not silently merge Howelton with Hamilton.
