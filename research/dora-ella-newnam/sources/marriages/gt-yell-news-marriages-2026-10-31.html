COCHRAN - PAGE
Wed at Danville
Miss Grace Page of Danville was married to Clarence B. Cochran at the home of the bride's parents Sunday. Only a few of the immediate friends were present. The bride is the daughter of Mr. and Mrs. H. R. Page of Danville and is a prominent member of the society set. She was an undergraduate nurse in Sparks Memorial Hospital at Fort Smith and was home on her vacation.
 Mr. Cochran, whose present home is in Sayre, Okla., was formerly a citizen of Danville, but has been attending the college of pharmacy in the University of Oklahoma, and at present is in the drug business in that state. After their honeymoon trip, which will include several of the larger Northern and Western cities, the young couple will be at home in Sayre. (Arkansas Democrat, Little Rock, Ark, Aug 29, 1922 - vm)

DURHAM - PERRY
Dardanelle, Ark., May 11 - Robert E. Durham of Hattiesburg, Miss., was married yesterday afternoon to Miss Aylmer E. Perry at the residence of the bride's father, Capt. James K. GRAVES - MOORE
Dardanelle, Ark. June 10 - Miss Edna Moore and Robert Graves, both of Delaware, were united in marriage in this city last evening at the First Methodist Church, Rev. C. W. Lester officiating. LANE - PERRY
Invitations have been issued for the marriage of Miss Marie Perry to John Lane, of this city. The wedding will be celebrated April 10 at the bride's home in Dardanelle, Ark. (The Courier-Journal, Louisville, Ky., Apr 7, 1901 - vm)

Wednesday afternoon at 1 o'clock a pretty wedding occurred at Dardanelle Ark., uniting Miss Marie Perry to John Lane of this city.
      The wedding took place at the beautiful country home of Capt. and Mrs. James Perry, which was decorated elaborately with many varieties of spring flowers, with Easter lilies predominant in the artistic array of blossoms.
      The attendants were: Miss Alymer Perry, the bride's sister, maid of honor; Eugene Stansbury of Memphis, best man; Miss Rosa Miller of Little Rock, Miss Louise Butler of Como, Miss; Miss Lela Howell of Dardanelle, Ark; bridesmaids; Moorhead Wright of Little Rock, Mace Howell and Arthur Perry of Dardanelle, groomsmen; Little Misses Annie Butler and Antonia Bernard ribbon bearers.
      The bride was beautifully gowned in white satin en traine with a tulle veil held in place with a wreath of orange blossoms. She carried bride roses.
      The bridesmaids wore white organdy and the maid of honor wore pink, all carrying bridesmaid roses.
      The ribbon bearers wore charming frocks of white organdy and added no little to the beauty of the weddign tableau as the Rev. W. M. Hays said the service.
      Among the guests from a distance were: The bride's aunt from Nashville, Mr. and Mrs. Hanks of Memphis and Mrs. Share of Little Rock.
      The bride and groom received a number of handsome and valuable presents, testifying in degree the popularity of the bride and groom.
      A reception was held from 2 to 3 o'clock, after which Mr. and Mrs. Lane left on their wedding journey to New Orleans and the gulf coast.
      After the 21st of this month they will live at 331 Vance Street. (The Commercial Appeal, Memphis, Tenn, Apr 12, 1901 - vm)
