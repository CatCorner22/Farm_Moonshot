Benjamin F. NEWNAM & Mary STEEL
Husband: age 26, born Owsley Co KY, residence Lee Co KY, miner, 1st marriage
Birthplace of Groom's Mother: Estill Co KY
Wife: age 25, born Owsley Co KY, residence Lee Co KY, 1st marriage
Birthplace of Bride's Father: NC
Birthplace of Bride's Mother: Estill Co KY
md: 15 Sept 1881 at Wm Steel's, Lee Co, by Robert Cole, M.G. of C.C.
witnesses: Wm H. Cole & Jeremiah Maloney

FAN on same book:
3 Sept 1880 Abner GUM & Sarah BENNET — witnesses Elihu Newnam & Frank Newnam.

Groom age 26 on 15 Sept 1881 ⇒ born ~1854–55 (1928 DC says 4 Oct 1853;
one-year slack common on licenses). Mother b. Estill matches Rhoda Tincher’s
Estill natal FAN. Do not merge with Luther Newnam’s father William × Katherine.
