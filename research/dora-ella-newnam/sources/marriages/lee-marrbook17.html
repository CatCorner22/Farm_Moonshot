(1923-1924)
Transcribed from photocopies of Lee County Marriage Book 17.  The records include the marriage affidavit (application) and the marriage return or certificate.
Even though a couple may have applied for a license in Lee County, they may have married elsewhere.
Transcribed by Debi Houser Kendrick.
