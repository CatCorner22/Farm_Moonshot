Smith - Stout

B/G is bride or groom but can possibly be incorrect. Dates are formatted as day, month, year. Some items are blank or missing (-) or (0) and some were impossible or hard to read (?). Some of the books, especially book #1, are in very bad condition with parts of the pages missing. This is the reason for the run of missing names on the first page of this transcription and scattered throughout the index.
