WWI Draft Registration Cards 1917 - 1918 Lee County Ky

## WWI Draft Registration Cards 1917 - 1918

The World War 1 draft had three separate registrations.

#1 - June 5, 1917 - Men ages 21 to 31 - born June 6, 1886 to June 5, 1896

#2 - June 5, 1918 - Men turning 21 since the last registration and a supplemental registration August 24, 1918.

#3 - September 12, 1918 - for men 18 to 21 and men 31 to 45 - men born 1872 to 1900.

All of the registrations used a different format of cards so the information contained on each card varies.

Many, many of these cards are signed only with an "X" noting his mark.

 There are many incorrectly spelled names or words and are transcribed as seen.
