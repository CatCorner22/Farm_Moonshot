Owsley County Courier, Friday, April 21, 1939
Rites Held For William Barrett
William BARRETT, age 79, died at the home of his daughter, Mrs. Leno
COMBS at Oil, Ky, Thursday, April 13. Death was due to heart trouble.
Funeral services were conducted Saturday by the Rev. Charlie KINCAID.
Enterment was in the family cemetery near Hopewell.
Survivers are: two sons, Walker and Fred of Clay City; two daughters,
Mrs. Leno COMBS, Oil, and Mrs. Charlie KINCAID, Zoe, and one brother,
John Bell BARRETT, Beattyville.
