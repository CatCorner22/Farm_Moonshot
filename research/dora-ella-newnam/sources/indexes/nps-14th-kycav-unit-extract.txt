opened 30 Oct 2026. Unit history, not an individual soldier card.

Companies "A," "B," "C" and "D" organized at Mt. Sterling, Ky., and
mustered in November 6, 1862. Other companies organized at Irvine, Ky.,
August 21, 1862, to February 13, 1863. Mustered out September 16, 1863,
to March 24, 1864.

Company-level window. Does not name Elias T. Newnam. Weighs toward a
September 1863 muster-out for Co C as a body; does not resolve the 16 Jun
vs 16 Sep conflict on his individual 1890 row. Do not average.
