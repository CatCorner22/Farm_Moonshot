Headnote (diplomatic): "Below we give the dates of several marriage licenses issued many years ago in Owsley county. These records were destroyed in the fire which burned the court house in Booneville." Notes left by J. W. F. Williams in Beattyville. This is a partial license list, not a complete county index.

Newman/Newnam/Tincher/Harrison grooms or brides on the published list: ONE row.
  21 July 1864  Harry H. Newnam and Mary J. Partington

1843-1850 portion of the same list (licenses dated 18 Apr 1843 through 6 Dec 1849): nil Newman/Newnam/Tincher/Harrison. The only Susan in the whole list is 8 Apr 1852 E. R. W. Cox and Susan Pitman (not Newman).

Not the original bond. Not a silent merge with 1860 H.H. Newman 15. Do not treat a nil on this partial list as a county-wide disproof of Harrison x Susan ~1848-50 (Owsley courthouse fire destroyed the books).
