IA item "beatty" (Beattyville Enterprise 1924-1932), OCR beatty_djvu.txt

digitized by UK Special Collections. OCR of the film; jp2 not cropped this
pass. Does NOT name parents. Does NOT replace vol 020 #09525.

1) Friday, 30 November 1928, Vol. XXII No. 48
   Masthead OCR: "BEATTYVILLE, LEE COUNTY, KENTUCKY, NOVEMBER 30, 1928"
   Local and Personal column (diplomatic from OCR, uncertain letters marked):

   "t'nc I. Doll Newman haa been verj *lek for a few days and r»>t able to
   be out."

   Reading: Uncle Doll Newman has been very sick for a few days and not
   able to be out. Thanksgiving locals on the same page. Nickname Doll +
   Beattyville residence in 1928. Uncertain letters in the OCR; claim
   capped if the reading were the only identifier — it is not.

2) Friday, 1 April 1932, Vol. XXVI No. 14 (Easter week; Easter Sunday was
   27 March 1932; "spent Easter" notes sit on this sheet, after the
   25 March 1932 masthead of No. 13)

   "E. T. Sparks of Hazard was visiting Uncle Doll Newnam in this city
   Tuesday."

   Alive in Beattyville 17 days before the 18 April 1932 death extract.
   Visitor E.T. Sparks of Hazard is unmerged FAN.
