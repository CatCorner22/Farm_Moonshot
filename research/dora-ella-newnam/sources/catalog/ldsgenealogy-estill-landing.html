Overview of Estill County Records

  • Estill County was created in 1808 from Clark and Madison counties.
  • Copies of birth and death certificates from 1911 and later can be obtained from the Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics.
  • The Estill County Clerk has land records starting in 1808, marriage records starting in 1808, burial records, and probate records starting in 1808.
  • The Estill County Clerk has divorce records and court records starting in 1808.
  • Online indexes and images of many of the above records can be found by using the links on this site.
