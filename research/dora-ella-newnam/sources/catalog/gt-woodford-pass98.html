County History

Woodford was surveyed and shaped from Fayette county in 1788 and Founded in 1789. It is the last of nine counties formed before Kentucky became a state in 1792.
The county is named for General William Woodford,
who was with General George Washington at Valley Forge.
Its county seat is Versailles. ..More
