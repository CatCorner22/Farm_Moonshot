About Kentucky Death Records

Some death records were kept by the government from 1852 to 1862, from 1874 to 1879, and from 1892 to 1910. Statewide registration of deaths began in 1911 with complete records by 1917. Copies of recent death certificates can be obtained from the Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics.
