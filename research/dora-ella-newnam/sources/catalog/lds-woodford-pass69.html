Overview of Woodford County Records

  • Woodford County was created in 1788 from Fayette County.
  • Copies of birth and death certificates from 1911 and later can be obtained from the Kentucky Office of Vital Statistics.
  • The Woodford County Clerk has land records starting in 1789, marriage records starting in 1789, and probate records starting in 1789.
  • The Woodford County Clerk Circuit Court has divorce records and court records starting in 1789.
  • Online indexes and images of many of the above records can be found by using the links on this site.
