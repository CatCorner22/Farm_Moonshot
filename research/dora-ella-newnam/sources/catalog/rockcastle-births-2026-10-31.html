Rockcastle County Births

The cut-off year for listing birth information will be 1923. This is the year that most other websites are using and will be respected here also.

1911 - 1923 Rockcastle County Births

Note: If a "1" follows a name, it's a male child of whoever's listed (usually the father's name, if known). If a "2" follows a name, it's a female child of the listed person. They used the numbers if the child hadn't been named when the birth certificate was filed. The other instance was if the child was stillborn or died before being named.
