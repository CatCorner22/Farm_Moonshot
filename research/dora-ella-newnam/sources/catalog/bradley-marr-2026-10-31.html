Partial Index to Bradley County, Arkansas Marriage Records before 1940

This index is for Bradley County, Arkansas marriage records prior to 1940. We need your help to build this index. Please send marriage records which you have collected, and remember that every additional record helps. Submitters' information is included (at the bottom of this page) along with the records to help you follow up on leads. (Note: We have tried to be faithful to the original records, especially with the spelling of names and when writing brides' names with or without the title "Miss".)

We do have some transcriptions of Marriages after 1940.

Some of the entries on the following pages were transcribed from records and indexes in the Bradley County courthouse. Please keep in mind that errors may have been made in the transcriptions or in typing the entries for the web site. Always check the original source yourself.

Marriage records that have a Correction will be noted as (C). You can read the corrections HERE.

If you have marriage records you would like to share, or if you have corrections to records listed here, please e-mail them to dlogan@alaska.net.

Many thanks to Jann Woodard for providing photocopies of marriage record index pages for volunteers to transcribe! These records are denoted by the text (for JW) after the submitter's name.

To protect others' privacy, please do not include living individuals in the records you submit.
