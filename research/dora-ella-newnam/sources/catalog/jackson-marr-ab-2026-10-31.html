compiled by Edie Smith           

In memory of my Ancestors. My parents, George Smith and Rosie Eastham. Dad was raised in Jackson Co., Ky. and Hamilton, Ohio. George Smith born May 24, 1894, son of Stratten and Caspanny Clemmons Smith.

Most of these were copied from the film that the LDS organization did of the Marriage bond books. Others were found in the courthouse, some were in the minister's return book that weren't in the marriage bonds books. others from my family records. Some from the census. Some long last names were shortened due to space but still recognizable. Some of the brides last names were found in the birth records only.
