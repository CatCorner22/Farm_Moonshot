Who We Are

Hi, my name is Gina Heffernan and I'm a Temporary County Coordinator. I'm very glad you stopped by and hope that you find this website useful for your genealogical research. I'm unable to help with specific research questions but, should you have any questions or comments regarding the Woodruff County Genealogy Project, please click the contact button up there and drop me a note.

This site is partnered with The ARGenWeb Project and The USGenWeb® Project. The records and files on these pages are FREE for your personal use and to help aid you in your research for tracing your roots!
 

Where We Are

Woodruff County is in the northeast quadrant of the State of Arkansas and is bounded in the north by Jackson County, to the east by Cross and St. Francis counties. Monroe County is to the south with Prairie County in the southwest corner and White County is our neighbor to the west. The county seat is Augusta.
 

What We Do

USGenWeb® and its affiliates are striving to make genealogical and historical records available on the Internet at no cost to our visitors. We can always use help with transcriptions, lookups, and county management. I started out just like you, looking for my family on the Internet.

I hope you find what you're looking for but, if not, please contact me with any comments or suggestions for the site so that I may better assist you in your research! If you're interested in adopting Woodruff or another county in The argenweb Project, check out the available counties and send an email to the argenweb State Coordinator with your request.

One last note, please understand that I don't live in Woodruff County and cannot do personal look-ups. Look-up requests can be made to individuals who have volunteered, check them out under Lookups.
