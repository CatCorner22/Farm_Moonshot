MYSTERIES




This stone was photographed in Scotland. It is an 18th century burial stone in good shape for a  Steven Rutledge. 

By chance my 2gfather's name was also Stephen Rutledge. 

Yet the connection between this stone and the Rutledge family of today, is same coat of arms depicted on this stone can be found on other websites for  Rutledge or Routledge families in three countries, and we all own the same exact registered Tartan.
