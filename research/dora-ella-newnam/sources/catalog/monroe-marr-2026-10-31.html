Jo Claire English has generously contributed her marriage books to be put online on Monroe County, Arkansas Project. The contents of these books were transcribed from records at the Monroe County Court House in Clarendon. The first of these books is Marriage Records, Monroe County Arkansas, Book B, 1850 - 1860. Many thanks to Jo Claire.
