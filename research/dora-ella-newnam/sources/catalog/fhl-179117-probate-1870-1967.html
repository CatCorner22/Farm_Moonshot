The item you selected, Probate records, 1870-1967, is located in the FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City, Utah.


How to Access a Copy of This Item


Step One: Find the item in the FamilySearch catalog

Here is the direct catalog entry for this item: https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/catalog/127913


Step Two: Determine if the item is a book or a microfilm

If the item is a book, it will have a book call number listed such as "974.67/W3 H2d".


If the item is a microfilm, it will have a microfilm number listed such as "United States and Canada Film 1234567".


Step Three: Determine how the item can be accessed


If the item is a microfilm it will have one or more of the following symbols:

Digital images are available by clicking on the camera image
Digital images are available, but only at a FamilySearch Center. To find a center near you, use the FamilySearch Center locator tool.
There is a searchable name index available
Full-text search is available. For more information about how to use full text search, see our article Unlocking FamilySearch: Mastering Full-Text Search
Record is on microfilm only and can only be viewed in the FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City, Utah

If the item is a book and is accessible online, it will have a link to the digital book on the FamilySearch website. If the item is a book and there is no link to view the book, it is not digitzed and can only be viewed in the FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City, Utah.


When the Item is Not Yet Digitized Online


When the item isn't online yet, you have a few options:

  • Search other library catalogs via WorldCat. It's possible that you can find the item in a library that is near you that you can visit to view the book, you may be able to request the item through interlibrary loan.
  • A Google search for the title of the item may also be helpful as the item could be digitized in another location.
  • Visit the FamilySearch Library in Salt Lake City, Utah. All of the books, microfilms, and other items are available to view free of charge to the public.
  • Hire a professional genealogist to research for you at the FamilySearch Library. Lists of genealogists are available through the Association of Professional Genealogists, the Board for Certification of Genealogists, and ICAPGen.
