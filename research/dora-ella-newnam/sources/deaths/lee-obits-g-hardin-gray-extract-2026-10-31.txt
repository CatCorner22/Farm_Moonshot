The Beattyville Enterprise
Thursday, 25 July 1946
Robert Hayes Gray, 68, died July 25, at 5:20 a.m. of a heart ailment...
He was a son of William and Mary Jane Brandenburg Gray, and was born in the Mount Eagle community April 14, 1876.
Survived by ... two brothers and two sisters, Mardin Gray, Arvel; Sidney Gray, Winston Salem, N.C.; Mrs. John Farmer, Mount Eagle community, and Mrs. Taulbee Smith, Cincinnati.

--- #vestamgray Vesta Mays GRAY ---
The Beattyville Enterprise
Thursday, 23 October 1958
Funeral services for Mrs. Vestie B. Grey, 76, were held at 10 a.m. Thursday, Oct. 23, at the grave by Rev. Lester Steele and Sam Watkins.
Mrs. Grey, the daughter of the late Larkin and Eliza Pendergrass Mays, was born in Lee county, Sept. 9, 1882 and died Oct. 21, at her home after a long illness. She was a member of the Church of Christ.
She is survived by her husband, H. K. Grey, Arvel; four sons, Roscoe and Hubert Grey, both of Arvel; Robert and Calvin Grey, both of Cincinnati, Ohio; five daughters, Mrs. Demia Mays, Arvel; Mrs. Gladys Lowery, Cincinnati, Ohio; Mrs. Gracie Reece, Camden, Ohio; Mrs. Ruby Fletcher, Cincinnati, Ohio, and Mrs. Cunigan, Arvel; one brother, Whitley Mays, Heidelberg; two sisters, Mrs. Ida Plowman, Cincinnati, Ohio, and Mrs. Bertie Ross, Leatherwood; 25 grandchildren and 11 great-grandchildren.
Burial was in the Grey cemetery with the Newnam Funeral Home in charge.
