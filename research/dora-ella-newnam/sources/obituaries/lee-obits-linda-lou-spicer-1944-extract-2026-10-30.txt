The Beattyville Enterprise
Thursday, 11 May 1944

Linda Lou Spicer, three-year-old daughter of Mr. and Mrs. Walker Spicer, died
Wednesday, May 3 at the St. Joseph Hospital in Lexington following an illness
of 10 days.

Besides her father, who is now serving with the U.S. Navy, survivors include the
mother, Mrs. Thelma Glass Spicer and a sister, Phyllis, 5, both of Lexington,
and the grandparents, Rev. and Mrs. Robert Spicer of Lower Buffalo.

Confirms a Robert Spicer couple at Lower Buffalo in May 1944 (same place as
Everett's 1939 sister "Mrs. Robert Spicer, Lower Buffalo"). Callie (Newnam)
Spicer's own death notice is still unread. Living 1944 children withheld beyond
the deceased granddaughter named here.
