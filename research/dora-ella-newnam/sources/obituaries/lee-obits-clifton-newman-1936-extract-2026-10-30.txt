The Berea Citizen
Thursday, 1 October 1936

Ed Newman, 20, was instantly killed, Clifton Newman, 17, and Raymond Adams, 20,
critically injured Monday afternoon at 2:45 o'clock when the truck they were
driving swerved and left the road just two and one half miles east of Berea on
Kentucky 21.

Clifton Newman died Tuesday afternoon at 4:00 o'clock ...

Ed is the son of Everett Newman of Idaymay, Lee County, and had been spending
the past few days with his uncle and aunt, Mr. and Mrs. Grant Newman. Clifton,
who died from concussion of the brain and chest injuries, is the son of
Mr. and Mrs. Grant Newman of Berea.

Funeral services for Clifton Newnam ... Methodist Church ... Burial ... Berea Cemetery.
Besides his mother and father, Clifton is survived by two brothers, Clayton and
David and by two sisters, Mabel and Marie.

The body of Ed Newnam was taken to the home of his parents in Lee County Tuesday
by the Rominger Funeral Home.
