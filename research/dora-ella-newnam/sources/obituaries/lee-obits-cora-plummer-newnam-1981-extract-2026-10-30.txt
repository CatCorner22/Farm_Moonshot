> > > > Cora Plummer NEWNAM 
> > > > 
> > > > Lexington Herald-Leader (Lexington, KY) Friday, 27 February 1981 
> > > > 
> > > > Cora P. Newnam, 87, of Beattyville, widow of Rev. Luther Newnam, died Wednesday, Central Baptist Hospital, Lexington of a short illness. 
> > > > 
> > > > She was a native of Lee County, a member of Beattyville Christian Church, member of Rainbow Council #129 Daughters of America; Beattyville Chapter #231, OES, and past state councilor of Kentucky Daughters of America. 
> > > > 
> > > > Survived by two daughters, Miss Eloise Newnam of Lexington, Mrs. Edwin (Betty Lou) McCroskey, Newburgh, Indiana; one son, Luther G. Newnam of Beattyville; one foster son Charles W. Plummer West Chester, Ohio; five grandchildren; five great-grandchildren. 
> > > > 
> > > > Funeral services 1 p.m. Saturday at the Newnam Funeral Home by Rev. Jerry Ginnan. Burial in Riverview Cemetery in Beattyville. Visitation at the funeral home after 4 p.m. Friday. 
> > > > 
> > > > Pallbearers will be the deacons of Beattyville Christian Church.
