The Beattyville Enterprise
Thursday, 14 December 1939

Everett Newnam, 50, Idamay, died in the Pattie A. Clay Infirmary, Richmond, at
2:30 o'clock Wednesday morning, December 13 of a bullet wound received Tuesday.

Nwnam [sic] was found on the Heidelberg road in a critical condition suffering from
a bullet wound in the body. The bullet was said to have struck Newnam
when he dropped a pistol accidently discharging it. He was brought
to Beattyville for first aid and then rushed to the hospital.

He is survived by two brothers, Frank Newnam, contractor of Berea and John Newnam
of Owsley County; one sister, Mrs. Robert Spicer, Lower Buffalo and three
daughters.
