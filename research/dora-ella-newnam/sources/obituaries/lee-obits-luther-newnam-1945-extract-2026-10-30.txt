> > > > Rev. Luther NEWNAM 
> > > > 
> > > > The Beattyville Enterprise Thursday, 1 March 1945 
> > > > 
> > > > Rev. Luther Newnam, 54, died at his home in Beattyville at 4:50 o'clock yesterday afternoon (Wednesday) following an illness of a few hours. He suffered a stroke at his hoome early yesterday morning. 
> > > > 
> > > > A descendant of Daniel Boone, Rev. Newnam was born in Beattyville February 21, 1891, the son of William and Katherine Sternberg Newnam. 
> > > > 
> > > > A member of the Beattyville Christian Church and a former pastor, Rev. Newnam had been pastor of Wades Mill Christian Church, Clark county, for the past 18 years and pastor of Bethlehem Christian Church, Clark county, for the past nine years. 
> > > > 
> > > > He was past state councilor and national representative of the Junior Order United Armerican mechanics and a member of Beattyville Council No. 7 of that organization. A veteran of World War I, he was a past commander and present adjutant of Lee County Post No. 84, the American Legion; financial secretary of Rainbow Council No. 129, Daughters of America; a past master and present secretary of Proctor Lodge No. 213, F.&A.M.; a member of Beattyville Chapter 231, Order of the Eastern Star; a member of the Royal Arch chapter at Clay City and a member of the Winchester Commandery. 
> > > > 
> > > > Rev. Newnam also served as grand chaplain of the Grand Lodge of Kentucky for the year 1943-'44. At the time of his death he had been associated with Congleton Bros. in Beattyville as bookkeeper for approximately 15 years. 
> > > > 
> > > > Funeral services will be held at the Beattyville Christian Church at two o'clock Saturday afternoon by Rev. Arthur T. Tipton of Ludlow, assisted by Rev. Sam Wilson and Rev. E. R. Sams. Burial will follow in Riverview cemetery. 
> > > > 
> > > > Survivors include his wief, Mrs. Cora Plummer Newnam, Beattyville; two daughters, Miss Betty Lou Newnam, Beattyville and Miss Eloise Newnam, Lexington; one son, Cpt. Luther G. Newnam, Fort Warren, Wyo.; one brother, Fred Newnam of Irvine, and one grandson. 
> > > >
