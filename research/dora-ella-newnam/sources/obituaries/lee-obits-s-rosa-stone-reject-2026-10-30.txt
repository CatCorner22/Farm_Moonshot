<p><b><font face="BibleScrT"><font color="#333333"><font size=+1>Williba,
Dec. 18 -- Mrs. Rosa Stone, 63 years old, died of heart trouble at her
home on Fraleys Creek, December 15, 1934.</font></font></font></b>
<p><b><font face="BibleScrT"><font color="#333333"><font size=+1>She was
born in Menifee County, she was married to Roland S. Stone in that county
and shortly afterwards came to this county about 40 years ago and have
been living there since.</font></font></font></b>
<p><b><font face="BibleScrT"><font color="#333333"><font size=+1>She was
ill only a short time and was for many years a member of the Baptist Church.</font></font></font></b>
<p><b><font face="BibleScrT"><font color="#333333"><font size=+1>Mrs. Stone
leaves surviving her Mrs. Price Little of Pittsburg, Pa., and Miss Lillian
Stone of Ashville, N.C.&nbsp; Two sons Robert of Hart county and Bill of
Fillmore.</
