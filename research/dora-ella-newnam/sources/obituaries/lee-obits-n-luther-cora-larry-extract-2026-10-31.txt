=== Cora Plummer NEWNAM (anchor corapnewnam) ===
Lexington Herald-Leader (Lexington, KY)
Friday, 27 February 1981
Cora P. Newnam, 87, of Beattyville, widow of Rev. Luther Newnam, died Wednesday,
Central Baptist Hospital, Lexington of a short illness.
She was a native of Lee County, a member of Beattyville Christian Church, member
of Rainbow Council #129 Daughters of America; Beattyville Chapter #231,
OES, and past state councilor of Kentucky Daughters of America.
Survived by two daughters, Miss Eloise Newnam of Lexington, Mrs. Edwin (Betty Lou)
McCroskey, Newburgh, Indiana; one son, Luther G. Newnam of Beattyville;
one foster son Charles W. Plummer West Chester, Ohio; five grandchildren;
five great-grandchildren.
Funeral services 1 p.m. Saturday at the Newnam Funeral Home by Rev. Jerry Ginnan.
Burial in Riverview Cemetery in Beattyville. Visitation at the funeral
home after 4 p.m. Friday.
Pallbearers will be the deacons of Beattyville Christian Church.

=== Larry Gene NEWNAM (anchor larrygnewnam) ===
The Beattyville Enterprise
Thursday, 18 February 1937
Larry Gene Newnam, three months, infant son of Rev. Luther and Cora Plummer Newnam,
died Saturday, February 18.
Funeral services were conducted in the Christian church by the pastors of the churches
in Beattyville: Rev. A. T. Tipton, Rev. F. J. Drew, and Rev. E. R. Sams.
Edward Rall, Eugene Rall, Elwood Lucas, and Truman Congleton, member of Mrs. Newnan's
Sunday School class were the pallbearers. The flowers were carried
by members of the Ladies Aid Society of the Christian church. Burial
was in the Riverview Cemetery.
NOTE: paper date Thursday 18 Feb 1937 vs “Saturday, February 18” — conflict #20.
Lee 1937 DC extract: b. 9 Nov 1936 Beattyville; d. 13 Feb 1937 Beattyville (that Saturday);
age 3 mos 4 ds; parents Luther Newnam & Cora Plummer. Do not average.

=== Rev. Luther NEWNAM (anchor luthernewnam) ===
The Beattyville Enterprise
Thursday, 1 March 1945
Rev. Luther Newnam, 54, died at his home in Beattyville at 4:50 o'clock yesterday afternoon
(Wednesday) following an illness of a few hours.  He suffered
a stroke at his hoome early yesterday morning.
A descendant of Daniel Boone, Rev. Newnam was born in Beattyville February 21, 1891,
the son of William and Katherine Sternberg Newnam.
A member of the Beattyville Christian Church and a former pastor, Rev. Newnam had
been pastor of Wades Mill Christian Church, Clark county, for the past
18 years and pastor of Bethlehem Christian Church, Clark county, for the
past nine years.
He was past state councilor and national representative of the Junior Order United
Armerican mechanics and a member of Beattyville Council No. 7 of that organization.
A veteran of World War I, he was a past commander and present adjutant
of Lee County Post No. 84, the American Legion; financial secretary of
Rainbow Council No. 129, Daughters of America; a past master and present
secretary of Proctor Lodge No. 213, F.&A.M.; a member of Beattyville
Chapter 231, Order of the Eastern Star; a member of the Royal Arch chapter
at Clay City and a member of the Winchester Commandery.
Rev. Newnam also served as grand chaplain of the Grand Lodge of Kentucky for the year
1943-'44.  At the time of his death he had been associated with Congleton
Bros. in Beattyville as bookkeeper for approximately 15 years.
Funeral services will be held at the Beattyville Christian Church at two o'clock
Saturday afternoon by Rev. Arthur T. Tipton of Ludlow, assisted by Rev.
Sam Wilson and Rev. E. R. Sams.  Burial will follow in Riverview cemetery.
Survivors include his wief, Mrs. Cora Plummer Newnam, Beattyville; two daughters,
Miss Betty Lou Newnam, Beattyville and Miss Eloise Newnam, Lexington; one
son, Cpt. Luther G. Newnam, Fort Warren, Wyo.; one brother, Fred Newnam
of Irvine, and one grandson.
