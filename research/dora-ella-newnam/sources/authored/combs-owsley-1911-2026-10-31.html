The Jackson Times, June 4, 1915, page 1 (Extracted by Louise Gaskill from Stephen Bowling's Homepage.

Wiley Combs Passes Away Youngest Son of Mrs. America Combs Victim of Tuberclosis-- Funeral Friday.

Wiley Combs, youngest son of Mrs. America Combs, died at his home in Jackson Thursday morning between four and five o'clock from tuberculosis. He was about twenty-six years of age. The body will be buried today in the Combs graveyard in this town. Some years ago his sister, Miss Nettie,one of the lovliest girls, was taken, followed a few years later by her brother, Hacker, and of the widowed there now remain only the widowed mother and one sone, Willie, a assenger conductor in the L. & N. service from here to McRoberts. They are fine people and have the sympathy of the entire town and county.
