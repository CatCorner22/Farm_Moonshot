1 Jan 1930. Mary F. TINCHER, mark.
Bequeaths all real and personal to niece Fannie HUNDLY and her husband
Walter HUNDLEY.
Attest: Geo. F. SMITH.
Witnesses: Geo. T. SMITH; Logan X BRANDENBURG (mark).
Proved 10 Oct 1932; confirmed / ordered recorded 14 Nov 1932 after 30 days.
Recorded Will Book No. 1, page 293 (Thomas C. PRYSE, Clerk;
Elizabeth MATTOCKS, D.C.; certificate 30 Nov 1932).

No Samuel P. Newnam. Testator is the 1932 Pebworth decedent whose parents
are John Newnam × Elizabeth Goe — not William × Rhoda.
