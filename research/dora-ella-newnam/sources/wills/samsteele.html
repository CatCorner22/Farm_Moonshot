Will of Samuel P. Steele



 

The Last Will and testament of Samuel P. STEEL of Beattyville, Kentucky.  Remindful of the fact that life is short and death is sure, I make and publish this my last will and testament.

First.  It is my will that all my just debts be paid.

Second.  I hereby revoke any and former wills heretofore made by me.

Third, I will and bequeth all of my personal property to my deat wife, Nannie STEEL.

Fourth, I devise all my lands and real estate wherever located to my Beloved wife Nannie STEEL with authority to sell dispose of by deed, of conveyence or otherwise by will.

Fifth.   I hereby appoint my beloved wife excutrix of this my last will and she is to act as such without electuting bond or without making any inventory of any of my property.

In testimony wherof I sign my name to the foregoing will in the presents of J. W. F. WILLIAMS old E. S. WILLIAMS attesting witnesses and tey signing their names in my presents.  This April 23 1928.

Samn P. STEELE

Attesting Witnesses
J. W. F. WILLIAMS
E. S. WILLIAMS

To the signature of Samule P. STEEL

State of Kentucky
County of Lee
I, Elizabeth MATTOCKS SIPPLE, Clerk Lee County Court, do hereby certify that an Instrument of writing purporting to be the Last Will and Testament of Samuel P. STEELE, having been produced and filed in open Court and same having been proven by the testimonies of J. W. F. WILLIAMS and E. S. WILLIAMS the two subscribing witnesses hereto, same was established by the Court to be the said Last Will and Testament of Samuel P. STEELE, deceased, and ordered to be recorded;  whereupon the same with the foregoing and this my certificate have been duly recorded in my said office in Will Book No. 1, page 493.

Given under my hand, this 15th day of March 1945.

Elizabeth MATTOCKS SIPPLE
Clerk Lee County Court
