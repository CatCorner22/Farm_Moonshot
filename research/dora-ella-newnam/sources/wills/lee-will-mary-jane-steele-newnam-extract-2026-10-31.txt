Will of Mary Jane NEWNAM of St. Helens, Lee County, Kentucky.
Dated 18 Nov 1943.
Residue (real, personal, mixed) to brother Sam P. STEELE — fee simple.
This Sam P. STEELE is NOT Samuel P. Newnam (P002).
Signed Mary Jane NEWNAM.
Witnesses: Brownlow STEELE, Beattyville; Mary STACEY, St. Helens.
Proved / filed open court 14 Feb 1944 (Elizabeth MATTOCKS, Clerk).
Recorded Will Book No. 1, page 472 (certificate 17 Mar 1944).

Published landing label: Jane Steele NEWMAN.
Matches 1944 death extract: Mary Jane Newnam d. 7 Feb 1944,
spouse Frank Newnam, father Wm Steele.
